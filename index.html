About Us

At Candid Art Studios, we believe every moment deserves to be captured with authenticity and artistry. Based in Cebu, our team specializes in weddings, pre-nup shoots, and event coverage. With a passion for storytelling through photography, we combine candid emotions with creative framing to deliver timeless memories.

Our mission is simple: to make your special day unforgettable by preserving genuine smiles, heartfelt emotions, and once-in-a-lifetime experiences.
